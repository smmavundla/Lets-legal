How to permanently delete your Let's account and data

This page explains how to delete your Let's account and all associated data. Let's is operated by Siyanda Mavundla, South Africa.

The fastest way — delete it yourself in the app:
  1. Open the Let's app and sign in.
  2. Go to the Profile tab.
  3. Scroll to the bottom to the Danger Zone.
  4. Tap Delete my account and confirm.
This permanently removes your account and data immediately. It cannot be undone.

Can't access the app?

If you can't sign in, email us at hello@letsapp.co.za from the email address linked to your account, with the subject line "Delete my account". We will verify your request and delete your account and data, usually within a few days and no later than 30 days.

What gets deleted

When your account is deleted, the following are permanently removed:

  • Your profile (display name, bio, Instagram handle)
  • Your profile photo and photo gallery
  • Your activity posts
  • Your messages in group and private chats
  • Your account login and email
  • Your notification token

What is kept

Nothing personal is retained after deletion. In rare cases, limited records may be kept only where required by law, and are not used for any other purpose.
